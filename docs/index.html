Podly

Battery, case and ear status for AirPods on Android. Free, no ads, no account, no internet access.

Tested on AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C). Other models follow public information about how AirPods report their state, so a feature can behave differently. In the app, Settings → Compatibility shows what has really worked on your earbuds and lets you send a short report about your model.
